🤼 Kabaddi Drills

Build raiding, defending, agility and raw strength with drills designed for young kabaddi warriors aged 8–18.

Toe Touch Raid

Raiding
Beginner

Master the signature kabaddi move by using flexibility and timing to score points against active defenders.

⏱ 20 min 🔄 15 attempts 👥 Partner
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Partner stands in defensive stance (one foot forward)
  2. Approach with short, quick steps while chanting
  3. Extend your leg and kick/touch the defender's foot
  4. Immediately retract and sprint to the baulk line
  5. Practice both left and right leg toe touches

💡 Coach Tip

The toe touch is all about hip flexibility and timing. Stretch your hip flexors daily to improve your reach.

Hand Touch Reflex

Raiding
Beginner

Develop quick hand touches while moving across the defense line to catch unwary defenders.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 20 raids 👥 Partner
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Defender stands near the baulk line
  2. Raider shuffles laterally along the line
  3. Launch a sudden forward lunge, extending the arm fully
  4. Tap the defender's shoulder or chest and retreat instantly

💡 Coach Tip

Do not overcommit your body weight forward, or you will lose balance and get caught in an ankle hold.

Dubki (Duck & Escape)

Raiding
Intermediate

Practice the famous "Dubki" to duck under a chain tackle and forcefully escape to your half.

⏱ 20 min 🔄 10 escapes 👥 Team
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Two defenders form a linked chain and advance rapidly
  2. Wait until the very last millisecond before impact
  3. Drop your hips and torso extremely low, ducking under their arms
  4. Drive forward powerfully using your legs to break through to the midline

💡 Coach Tip

The Dubki relies on leg strength and keeping your eyes open. If you close your eyes, you will lose orientation.

Bonus Point Precision

Raiding
Intermediate

Train specifically to cross the bonus line with one foot while dragging the trailing foot in the air.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 20 attempts 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Set up a physical line to act as the bonus line
  2. Approach the line dynamically
  3. Step across the line with your lead foot, planting it firmly
  4. Simultaneously drag or lift your trailing foot in the air
  5. Push off the lead foot to return quickly safely

💡 Coach Tip

The trailing foot must be completely in the air for the bonus rule to trigger. Dragging the toe will invalidate it.

Kick & Turn (Back Kick)

Raiding
Intermediate

Use a back kick to surprise trailing defenders who try to tackle you from behind as you retreat.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 15 kicks 👥 Partner
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Raid deep into the opponent's corner
  2. As you turn your back to sprint to the midline, sense the defender chasing
  3. Without looking, throw a powerful back kick like a mule
  4. Use the momentum of the kick to propel yourself back to the midline

💡 Coach Tip

Spatial awareness is key here. You must know exactly how close the defender is without turning your head.

Ankle Hold Practice

Defending
Beginner

Learn the fundamental ankle hold. Practice timing your dive and gripping the raider's ankle firmly.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 20 holds 👥 Partner
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Raider approaches the corner position
  2. Wait for the raider to lift their foot for a touch or bonus
  3. Dive explosively with both arms extended
  4. Wrap both hands securely around the ankle, pulling it into your chest
  5. Keep your head away from the raider's knees

💡 Coach Tip

Grip is everything. Aim for the skinniest part of the ankle securely above the shoe, linking your fingers if possible.

Corner Chain Link

Defending
Beginner

Practice moving simultaneously with your corner partner to maintain a strong defensive chain.

⏱ 10 min 🔄 5 mins 👥 Partner
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Link hands tightly with another defender
  2. Shuffle left and right along the defensive formation
  3. Maintain the exact same distance; do not let the chain break
  4. When the raider attacks, move as one solid unit to block

💡 Coach Tip

Communication is vital. The corner decides when to advance; the inner defender must follow instantly.

Thigh Hold & Block

Defending
Intermediate

Execute a powerful mid-body tackle targeting the raider's thighs to completely halt their momentum.

⏱ 20 min 🔄 15 holds 👥 Partner
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Wait for the raider to turn slightly or cross your zone
  2. Step in aggressively, driving your shoulder into their upper thigh
  3. Wrap both arms tightly around both thighs, locking them together
  4. Drive them sideways or backwards away from the midline

💡 Coach Tip

A high thigh hold is much harder for a raider to escape from than a low knee hold. Pin their legs together.

Dash (Running Block)

Defending
Intermediate

The cover defender sprints across the court to forcefully push the raider off the mat.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 10 dashes 👥 Team
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Raider attacks the opposite corner deeply
  2. Cover defender identifies the opportunity and sprints laterally
  3. Make contact with the raider's side/ribs using the shoulder and straight arms
  4. Use sheer momentum to carry both of you out of bounds

💡 Coach Tip

Timing is crucial. If you dash too early, the raider will side-step you. Commit at maximum velocity.

Diving Double Knee Hold

Defending
Intermediate

A surprise attack from the cover position, diving to lock both knees of a retreating raider.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 12 dives 👥 Partner
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Wait until the raider pivots to retreat to the midline
  2. Launch a low, horizontal dive right behind their legs
  3. Wrap your arms tightly around both knees
  4. Use your body weight to pull them down instantly

💡 Coach Tip

Surprise is your only weapon here. The raider must not see you coming until you already have their knees locked.

Lateral Shuffle Drill

Agility
Beginner

Build side-to-side speed crucial for both raiding and defending. Improve footwork and change-of-direction.

⏱ 10 min 🔄 4 sets 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Place two cones 10 metres apart
  2. Start in a low defensive stance at one cone
  3. Shuffle quickly sideways to the other cone without crossing your feet
  4. Touch the cone and immediately shuffle back
  5. Repeat continuously for 45 seconds

💡 Coach Tip

Keep your center of gravity low. The lower you are, the faster you can change direction.

T-Cone Drill

Agility
Beginner

Improve multi-directional agility by sprinting forward, shuffling sideways, and backpedaling rapidly.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 8 reps 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Set up 4 cones in a T-shape (10m forward, 5m left and right)
  2. Sprint forward from base to the center cone
  3. Sidestep to the left cone, then sidestep all the way to the right cone
  4. Sidestep back to center, then backpedal to the base

💡 Coach Tip

Focus on explosive transitions at the cones. Do not round off the corners; plant your foot and cut sharply.

Frog Jumps (Leg Power)

Agility
Intermediate

Build the immense lower body power required for sudden leaps, kicks, and diving tackles.

⏱ 10 min 🔄 3 sets of 15m 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Squat down deep until your hands touch the floor
  2. Explode forward and upward as far as possible
  3. Land softly back into the deep squat position
  4. Immediately rebound into the next jump

💡 Coach Tip

Use your arms to throw yourself forward. Land quietly on the balls of your feet to protect your knees.

React & Turn Sprints

Agility
Intermediate

Replicate the scenario of a raider turning to escape, requiring instant reaction to auditory cues.

⏱ 10 min 🔄 10 sprints 👥 Partner
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Lie face down on the ground, or sit with your back to the direction of travel
  2. Partner blows a whistle or shouts "GO"
  3. Scramble to your feet, turn 180 degrees, and sprint 15 meters at max speed
  4. Walk back to recover

💡 Coach Tip

The ability to accelerate from a static or disadvantaged position is what separates good raiders from great ones.

Shadow Raiding Footwork

Agility
Intermediate

Perform a continuous free-flow raiding sequence visualizing defenders attacking from all angles.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 5 x 30 sec 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Start at the midline, chanting "Kabaddi, Kabaddi"
  2. Perform quick foot taps, fake lunges, and side steps randomly
  3. Visualize dodging ankle holds and executing toe touches
  4. Maintain the chant without running out of breath for 30 seconds

💡 Coach Tip

This is not just an agility drill but a cardio respiratory test. Never stop chanting.

Plank and Core Holds

Strength
Beginner

Develop core stability which is the foundation of preventing injuries during violent tackles.

⏱ 10 min 🔄 3 x 1 min 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Get into a forearm plank position on the mat
  2. Ensure your body forms a perfectly straight line from head to heels
  3. Brace your core tightly as if preparing to take a punch
  4. Hold for 60 seconds without letting your hips sag

💡 Coach Tip

A strong core transfers power from your lower body to your upper body when executing a tackle or an escape.

Farmer's Walk (Grip Strength)

Strength
Beginner

Kabaddi requires iron-like grip strength for ankle, thigh, and wrist holds. This drill builds that endurance.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 4 x 40m 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Pick up two extremely heavy dumbbells, kettlebells, or sandbags
  2. Squeeze the handles as aggressively as possible
  3. Walk 40 meters while maintaining perfectly upright posture
  4. Rest for 60 seconds and repeat until your forearms burn

💡 Coach Tip

Do not let the weights rest against your legs. The grip must do all the work.

Wrestler's Bridge

Strength
Intermediate

Strengthen the neck and spinal erectors to survive heavy dashes and awkward landing falls securely.

⏱ 10 min 🔄 3 x 30 sec 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Lie on your back on a soft mat
  2. Bridge up onto your feet and the crown of your head
  3. Arch your back fully, removing your hands from the floor if possible
  4. Hold the static position, breathing normally

💡 Coach Tip

Proceed with extreme caution. If you are new to this, keep your hands on the mat to share the weight with your neck.

Partner Dragging

Strength
Intermediate

Build functional posterior chain strength by physically dragging a resisting partner backward.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 5 runs 👥 Partner
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Partner sits on the floor or provides heavy resistance while standing
  2. Grab their wrists or harness securely
  3. Lean backward, driving powerfully through your legs (like a reverse sprint)
  4. Drag them 10 meters backward to simulate escaping a tackle

💡 Coach Tip

Use short, incredibly powerful steps. Do not try to pull with your arms—pull with your quads and glutes.

Overhead Medicine Ball Throws

Strength
Intermediate

Develop explosive whole-body power for throwing raiders off you or dashing them out of bounds.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 4 sets of 10 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Hold a heavy medicine ball (5-10kg) with both hands
  2. Squat down, bringing the ball between your legs
  3. Explode upward, extending your hips, knees, and ankles
  4. Throw the ball backward over your head as far as possible

💡 Coach Tip

Maximum power generation only happens when your entire body works in sequence: legs, hips, back, then arms.
