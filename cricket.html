🏏 Cricket Drills

Master batting, bowling, fielding and fitness with drills designed for young cricketers aged 8–18.

Shadow Batting

Batting
Beginner

Practice your stance and bat swing without a ball. Focus on footwork, head position, and follow-through for each shot.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 3 sets 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Take your batting stance in front of a mirror or open area
  2. Practice front-foot defence 20 times — focus on leading with your head
  3. Practice back-foot punch 20 times — transfer weight smoothly
  4. Play cover drive motions 20 times — ensure full bat swing
  5. Repeat pull shot, cut shot, and straight drive sequences

💡 Coach Tip

Keep your eyes level and head still. Imagine a real bowler while practising — visualisation builds muscle memory faster.

Cone Balance Drill

Batting
Beginner

Improve your base and balance by batting while standing between two cones to restrict wide unwanted movements.

⏱ 10 min 🔄 5 overs 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Place two cones shoulder-width apart around your stance
  2. Do stationary drives without knocking over the cones
  3. Focus strictly on head alignment over the front knee
  4. Gradually increase swing speed while maintaining balance

💡 Coach Tip

Balance is everything in batting. Your head should always lead the movement, not your hands.

Throw-Down Hitting

Batting
Intermediate

Face throw-downs from a partner at varying pace and length. Focus on timing, shot selection, and playing straight.

⏱ 20 min 🔄 5 sets of 12 👥 Partner
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Set up in the nets with full batting gear
  2. Partner throws from 16 yards at moderate pace
  3. First set: play only defensive shots
  4. Second set: drive every ball on the ground
  5. Third set: mix attacking and defending naturally

💡 Coach Tip

Watch the ball from the hand. Don't pre-decide your shot — react to the length and play accordingly.

One-Handed Drives

Batting
Intermediate

Use only your top hand or bottom hand independently to build specific forearm strength and bat control.

⏱ 10 min 🔄 30 balls 👥 Partner
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Take your stance holding the bat with only your top hand
  2. Have a partner toss the ball gently underarm
  3. Drive the ball focusing on maintaining a high elbow
  4. Switch to bottom hand only for power hitting practice

💡 Coach Tip

Your top hand controls the direction of the shot, while your bottom hand controls the power.

Spin Sweep Practice

Batting
Intermediate

Dedicated practice for playing the sweep shot against spinners to counter turning deliveries.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 30 balls 👥 Partner
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Stretch your front leg out fully towards the pitch of the ball
  2. Keep your head completely still and over your front knee
  3. Bring the bat from high to low across the line of the ball
  4. Roll your wrists to keep the ball on the ground

💡 Coach Tip

Never pre-meditate the sweep. Only play it to balls pitched on a good length around leg stump.

Target Bowling

Bowling
Beginner

Bowl at a target cone placed on a good length spot. Focus on run-up rhythm, release point, and consistent line.

⏱ 20 min 🔄 6 overs 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Place a target cone on a good length at off-stump line
  2. Mark your run-up starting point
  3. Bowl 6 balls per over, aiming to hit the cone
  4. Track how many times you hit or pass close to the target
  5. Move the target to different lengths between overs

💡 Coach Tip

Consistency > speed. Focus on hitting the same spot repeatedly before increasing your pace.

Wall Bowling Rhythm

Bowling
Beginner

Bowl against a wall or net from a short distance to purely focus on your bowling action and follow-through.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 40 balls 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Stand 5 meters from a wall or heavy net
  2. Take a 3-step run-up
  3. Bowl with full action but moderate pace
  4. Focus on pulling your non-bowling arm down aggressively
  5. Ensure your back leg comes all the way through

💡 Coach Tip

Your non-bowling arm acts like a steering wheel. Pull it down hard to generate momentum.

Crease Variety Drill

Bowling
Intermediate

Practice delivering the ball from different widths of the crease to change the angle of delivery.

⏱ 20 min 🔄 5 overs 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Over 1: Bowl close to the stumps
  2. Over 2: Bowl from the middle of the crease
  3. Over 3: Bowl wide of the crease
  4. Over 4 & 5: Mix the angles randomly
  5. Aim to hit the same good length regardless of the starting angle

💡 Coach Tip

Bowling wide of the crease creates an angle in to the batsman, making LBW or bowled more likely.

Slower Ball Mastery

Bowling
Intermediate

Develop variations by practicing off-cutters, leg-cutters, and back-of-the-hand slower deliveries.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 4 overs 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Maintain the exact same arm speed as your fast delivery
  2. Roll your fingers down the side of the ball for an off-cutter
  3. Bowl 12 cutters, focusing on length
  4. Practice the back-of-the-hand slower ball for 12 deliveries

💡 Coach Tip

The secret to a good slower ball is arm speed deception. Fake the fast ball, but change the grip at the last millisecond.

Yorker Execution

Bowling
Intermediate

Nail the blockhole by practicing bowling full deliveries at the base of the stumps.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 30 balls 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Place a shoe or cone directly on the batting crease
  2. Aim to hit the object with every delivery
  3. Release the ball slightly later than your standard delivery
  4. Keep your eyes locked on the base of the stumps

💡 Coach Tip

Don't bowl the yorker by looking down early. Look at the batsman's toes during release.

Ground Fielding Circuit

Fielding
Beginner

Improve ground fielding with a circuit that covers pick-up-and-throw, diving stops, and underarm hits.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 4 rounds 👥 Team
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Set up 4 stations in a square: attack, pick-up, dive, throw
  2. Station 1: Charge at the ball, pick up on the move and throw
  3. Station 2: Field a rolling ball off your weaker side
  4. Station 3: Dive to stop a wide throw, recover, and throw
  5. Station 4: Underarm hit the stumps from 10 metres

💡 Coach Tip

Get low — bent knees and soft hands are the foundation of great fielding. Watch the ball into your hands.

Underarm Direct Hits

Fielding
Beginner

Practice close-in fielding by retrieving the ball and accurately throwing underarm at single stumps.

⏱ 10 min 🔄 20 throws 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Place a single stump 10 meters away
  2. Roll the ball out softly in front of you
  3. Run in, pick up with one hand outside your front foot
  4. Use an underarm flick to hit the stump while moving

💡 Coach Tip

Keep your throwing arm straight and point your lead shoulder at the target during the flick.

High Catch Practice

Fielding
Intermediate

Practice taking high catches with a partner using a sling or high throws. Build confidence under pressure.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 20 catches 👥 Partner
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Partner hits or throws the ball high into the air (30+ meters)
  2. Sprint to get under the ball quickly
  3. Form a cup with your hands at eye level (reverse cup or standard)
  4. Cushion the ball softly into your chest as you catch it

💡 Coach Tip

Never take your eyes off the ball while it is in the air. Keep your hands soft to absorb the impact.

Slip Cordon Reflexes

Fielding
Intermediate

Enhance your reaction time in the slips using a deflection board or by having a partner edge the ball.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 30 catches 👥 Partner
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Stand in a low, athletic crouch with hands ready at knee height
  2. Partner throws the ball hard against a deflection board or wall
  3. React instantly to catch the deflected ball
  4. Stay low! Most slip catches go down rather than up.

💡 Coach Tip

Keep your weight on the balls of your feet. If you are resting on your heels, you won't react fast enough.

Boundary Relay Throws

Fielding
Intermediate

Simulate chasing a boundary ball, sliding to stop it, and throwing a flat, hard return over the stumps.

⏱ 20 min 🔄 15 runs 👥 Team
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Sprint 30 meters to chase a ball rolling towards the boundary
  2. Execute a sliding stop using your shin and knee
  3. Pop up instantly using your momentum
  4. Throw a flat, one-bounce return to the wicketkeeper

💡 Coach Tip

When sliding, tuck your lead leg underneath and slide on the side of your calf and thigh to avoid injury.

Crease Sprints (Shuttle Runs)

Fitness
Beginner

Build the explosive speed and turning ability required for running between the wickets.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 10 sprints 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Mark out a distance of 20 meters (pitch length)
  2. Sprint from one end to the other with your bat
  3. Slide your bat over the line, turn sharply, and sprint back (a "two")
  4. Rest for 30 seconds and repeat 10 times

💡 Coach Tip

Always turn facing the ball (or the side where the ball would normally be hit) so you don't run blind.

Core Medicine Ball Twists

Fitness
Beginner

Develop the rotational core strength essential for powerful batting and bowling.

⏱ 10 min 🔄 3 sets of 20 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Sit on the ground with knees bent, holding a medicine ball
  2. Lean back slightly to engage your core, lifting feet off the floor
  3. Twist your torso to touch the ball to the ground on your left
  4. Twist rapidly to touch the ball on your right

💡 Coach Tip

The power for big hits comes from your core and hips, not just your arms. Keep movements explosive.

Bowler's Bound Variations

Fitness
Intermediate

Enhance your bowling run-up momentum with plyometric bounding exercises to build leg power.

⏱ 15 min 🔄 4 sets of 20m 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Perform exaggerated bounding strides over 20 meters
  2. Drive your knee high and push off forcefully with your back leg
  3. Use your arms actively to generate vertical and forward momentum
  4. Walk back to the start to recover

💡 Coach Tip

Focus on maximum air time and stride length rather than speed.

Reaction Ball Agility

Fitness
Intermediate

Use an unpredictable reaction ball to improve footwork agility and hand-eye coordination.

⏱ 10 min 🔄 5 minutes 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Drop or throw a 6-sided reaction ball against a wall
  2. React quickly to catch it before it bounces twice
  3. Stay low in an athletic stance throughout the drill
  4. Increase the throw speed as you get better

💡 Coach Tip

Don't try to guess where the ball will go. Stay relaxed, balanced, and let your reflexes take over.

Shoulder Resistance Bands

Fitness
Intermediate

Strengthen the rotator cuff and shoulder muscles to increase throwing distance and prevent injuries.

⏱ 10 min 🔄 3 sets of 15 👤 Solo
▶ Watch on YouTube 📺

Steps

  1. Attach a resistance band to a fence at shoulder height
  2. Perform internal and external rotations with your throwing arm
  3. Perform simulated throwing motions against the band's resistance
  4. Focus on a slow, controlled release (eccentric movement)

💡 Coach Tip

Cricket places terrible strain on the rotator cuff. Daily band work is the best way to extend your career.
